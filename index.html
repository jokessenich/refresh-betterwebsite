Questions

Before you ask.

Is the mockup actually free?+
Yes. No credit card, no obligation, no hidden catch. We'll redesign your homepage as a mockup so you can see exactly what's possible. If you want to move forward, great. If not, you still walk away with a professional design concept you can reference.
What happens each month after launch?+
We pull your real analytics — visitor behavior, bounce rates, conversion paths, keyword rankings — and identify the single highest-impact improvement. We build it, stage it for you, and send you a summary. You tap "Make It Live" and it goes to production. The whole thing takes about 30 seconds of your time.
How do I approve changes?+
You'll get an email with a clear summary of what changed and why. It includes a preview link and a one-tap "Make It Live" button. No logins, no dashboards, no meetings. If you want changes, just reply to the email.
What if I don't like an update?+
Nothing goes live without your approval. If you don't like the direction, just tell us. We'll adjust it or skip that month and try something different next time. You're always in control.
How long does a full website build take?+
Most sites launch in about 2 weeks from the start of the design phase. The mockup comes first — free in 48 hours. If you love it and sign up, we move straight into the full build. We'll give you a clear timeline and stick to it.
Is this a template or fully custom?+
Fully custom. Every design is built from scratch based on your brand, your audience, and your goals. We don't reskin templates and call it strategy. Your site will look and feel like yours — because it is.
Are there any upfront costs?+
None. Your subscription covers everything — design, development, hosting, SSL, monthly improvements, and support. No setup fees, no deposits, no surprise invoices. Cancel anytime.
